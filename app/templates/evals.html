What the numbers mean

  • Correct: matches the owner's true fact, or left empty because the owner didn't know it.
  • Invented: a field filled that the owner never could have stated: a fact they didn't know, or a question that doesn't apply to their business. In insurance a made-up answer can void a policy, so this matters more than accuracy.
  • Wrong / missed: the owner knew it, and the agent recorded something else / nothing.
  • Accuracy counts every field that applies to the business, except the free-text description. Simulated customers are easier than real people and occasionally state facts they were told they don't know, so the transcripts are published below to be read.
